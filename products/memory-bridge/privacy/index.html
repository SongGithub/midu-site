Privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026

Midu, operated by Song Jin in Australia, provides Memory Bridge. This policy covers the Memory Bridge service, including early access, accounts and connected assistants.

Information we collect and why

  • Early access and invitations. We collect the email address you submit, the optional description of what you want to try, and the request and invitation status. We use those details to review requests, issue invitations and help with access.
  • Account and sign-in information. Amazon Cognito manages sign-in, including email and password authentication. When you choose Google sign-in, Google supplies an account identifier, your email address and whether that address is verified. Cognito uses these details to authenticate you. Memory Bridge uses the resulting account identifier to link your sign-in to the right workspace. We use Google data for sign-in, account linking and security.
  • Workspace information. We store the notes you save and the settings and usage records needed to run your workspace. We use your notes to retrieve information you request and apply your selected assistant permissions.
  • Operational and support information. We maintain service and usage records to protect accounts, enforce limits, troubleshoot problems and respond to support requests. Session cookies keep you signed in and help protect the sign-in flow.

Service providers and connected assistants

We use Amazon Web Services to run Memory Bridge, authenticate accounts and deliver invitation emails. The core workspace service is hosted in Sydney, Australia. External sign-in providers and connected assistants may process information outside Australia under their own privacy policies. Their processing locations depend on the provider and how you use it.

When you connect an assistant, it can receive information from your workspace under the permissions you grant. You can revoke its access in Memory Bridge. Information an assistant already received is subject to that provider’s privacy and retention settings.

Memory Bridge uses Google account details for authentication. Your workspace notes are not shared with Google as part of Google sign-in. We do not sell Google account details or use them for advertising.

Retention and your choices

You can export your notes, revoke an assistant’s access or delete your workspace from Memory Bridge. Workspace deletion removes your workspace data from the service. Sign-in records, invitation records and operational logs are handled separately. We keep them as needed for service administration, security, support and legal obligations.

To request access to or correction of your personal information, ask about deletion of other records, or raise a privacy concern, use the Midu contact form and mention Memory Bridge. We may verify your identity before responding. Please leave passwords and invitation or recovery codes out of your message.
