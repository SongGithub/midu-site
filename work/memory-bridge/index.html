Memory Bridge by Midu · Case study

Your knowledge stays with you when your AI changes

Memory Bridge gives compatible AI assistants a shared record of your facts, decisions and lessons. When you switch assistants, you can reuse that context and keep control of what you share.

Explore Memory Bridge and request early access

Save context once and reuse it across assistants

Keep preferences, decisions and the reasons behind them in one place. An assistant you connect can retrieve relevant notes and point to its sources. You can correct the record and carry it with you in Markdown.

Review what your assistant saves

After a task, an assistant can propose a note about what happened and what to try next. You review which notes become part of your trusted record, and a later session can refer to the approved lesson.

For example, if you settle on a format for weekly project updates, you can save that decision and ask a different assistant to use it when drafting next week’s update.

Choose which assistants get access

Memory Bridge connects to compatible assistants through the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Each connection needs its own approval. Pilot onboarding includes checking that your assistant can connect and helping you retrieve your first saved note.

How well does it find the right note?

In a benchmark of 33 queries against fictional notes, Memory Bridge ranked the expected note first for 29 queries and in the top three for all 33. The benchmark measured note retrieval. Answer quality and time saved in everyday use still need pilot evidence.
