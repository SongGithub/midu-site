About

Curious about the work behind the tools.

I’ve spent years working through Kubernetes operations, monitoring, CI/CD, and cloud infrastructure. I like getting close to the real workflow: where information gets lost, where a deployment becomes fragile, and where a small tool can remove repeated effort.

That curiosity now extends to AI. I’m interested in grounded knowledge retrieval and useful automation, with testing and clear failure paths built in.

Outside work, I love endurance running. It keeps me fit, fits around my day, and gets me outdoors for sunshine and fresh air. It’s a welcome balance to desk work. I also tinker with tools for runners, including a shoe mileage tracker built around Strava and Garmin. You can find me on Strava.
